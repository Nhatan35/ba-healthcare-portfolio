How I Analyze

A proposed analytical approach, not a record of completed project phases.

Understand

What needs to change?

Clarify the context, the people affected, and the evidence behind the problem.

Connect

Where does the work meet?

Map the workflow, the handoffs, and the rules that shape each decision.

Define

What should happen?

Turn needs into scoped requirements, explicit exceptions, and viable options.

Validate

How would we know?

Set testable criteria and distinguish expected value from observed results.
