## UX-01 — Concrete C2 recruiter-facing storytelling

- **Priority:** P1
- **Status:** NOT STARTED
- **Problem / observation:** The accepted anonymous professional draft describes owner-attested activities, but no permitted concrete analytical example has been inspected to demonstrate the reasoning behind a specific contribution.
- **User value:** Recruiters and hiring managers can assess a bounded contribution and its rationale while understanding the strength and limits of its evidence.
- **Proposed improvement:** Once a permitted example exists, develop a concise account of the context, analytical question, personal action, rationale, actual disposition and remaining uncertainty.
- **Dependencies:** A permitted example or sanitized summary; owner clarification of personal/team boundaries; exact source/revision and review decision; later editorial and implementation authorization.
- **Evidence / confidentiality constraints:** Use existing claim references and collection slots without treating them as verified artifacts. Do not reconstruct confidential workflows, disclose private API details, invent decisions or equate coordination with code authorship. Public wording requires separate disclosure clearance; any synthetic example must be clearly distinct from professional evidence.
- **Measurable acceptance criteria:**
  1. At least one permitted example records its source/revision, contribution boundary, review state and allowed use.
  2. Every factual sentence in the proposed example maps to an allowed source or explicitly identified owner testimony; unsupported conclusions are omitted or marked unresolved.
  3. The example distinguishes the need, analysis, rationale and actual disposition; unknown validation/outcomes are stated, with no invented metric or stakeholder decision.
  4. Owner review of the exact draft and a separate evidence/permission review are recorded before any authorized runtime incorporation; neither is implied by writing the draft.
- **Future implementation scope, if later authorized:** C2 summary/detail storytelling and consistent references on its existing surfaces. Keep its ID, slug and URL; no new case identity or automatic publication.

## UX-02 — Accurate professional About/Profile

- **Priority:** P1
- **Status:** NOT STARTED
- **Problem / observation:** The professional profile requires accurate, owner-confirmed career information; intended positioning alone does not establish employment, seniority or qualifications.
- **User value:** Recruiters can understand the owner's relevant background, current focus and contribution without interpreting placeholders as facts.
- **Proposed improvement:** Prepare a concise professional biography and structured career facts from permitted owner-confirmed information.
- **Dependencies:** Owner-confirmed roles, dates, background, target positioning and disclosure limits; permitted supporting sources where available; exact-copy review and later implementation scope.
- **Evidence / confidentiality constraints:** Do not invent employment, qualifications, seniority, proficiency or achievements. Keep owner confirmation distinct from independent corroboration. Omit confidential organization or client details and unnecessary personal data.
- **Measurable acceptance criteria:**
  1. Every career claim has a recorded source and owner confirmation of its exact wording; gaps are omitted or honestly described.
  2. Profile facts agree with the proposed resume and project contribution boundaries; all identified contradictions are resolved or explicitly withheld from use.
  3. The approved draft contains zero unresolved authoring placeholders and zero unsupported credentials or achievement metrics.
  4. The owner records the exact biography revision and allowed audience; any public use separately meets disclosure gates.
- **Future implementation scope, if later authorized:** Existing About/Profile content and shared profile data, with only the presentation changes necessary for the approved facts. New routes require explicit scope.

## UX-03 — Public-safe Resume and Contact content

- **Priority:** P1
- **Status:** NOT STARTED
- **Problem / observation:** Recruiters need a usable resume and contact path; content and channels must be deliberately supplied and approved rather than inferred from private records.
- **User value:** Readers can obtain the intended professional summary and reach the owner through a chosen channel.
- **Proposed improvement:** Prepare a minimized resume and owner-selected contact content, with accurate availability states and functional destinations when authorized.
- **Dependencies:** Confirmed career facts, owner-selected contact details/channels, permitted resume revision, privacy review and later implementation authorization.
- **Evidence / confidentiality constraints:** Do not copy private contact details from intake material. Remove unnecessary identifiers and inspect file metadata; public-safe preparation does not itself authorize publishing or sending messages. No confidential employment/client material or fabricated achievements.
- **Measurable acceptance criteria:**
  1. Every resume fact matches the confirmed profile register or has a documented reason for different scope; no unresolved placeholders remain in the proposed release copy.
  2. Every intended contact channel and resume revision has explicit owner approval for its audience and use.
  3. A recorded review covers visible content and export metadata, with zero unresolved sensitive-data findings in the proposed package.
  4. If later implemented, every displayed contact/download action resolves to its approved destination and is keyboard accessible; unavailable content has honest text instead of a nonfunctional action. Testing does not send unsolicited messages.
- **Future implementation scope, if later authorized:** Resume asset/link, Contact content and existing availability states. Forms, delivery services, analytics, new routes and outreach require separate scope.

## UX-04 — Preparation checklist readability

- **Priority:** P2
- **Status:** NOT STARTED
- **Problem / observation:** The existing 19-section checklist already uses a collapsed disclosure. Its length and nested content create a further readability review opportunity; no measured usability failure is asserted.
- **User value:** Readers can scan the overview and find detailed preparation evidence without losing context or access to missing-section explanations.
- **Proposed improvement:** Review summaries, navigation and progressive disclosure while preserving the complete canonical structure and evidence visibility.
- **Dependencies:** Baseline inventory of section IDs, titles, order, nested sections and anchors; approved interaction scope; later keyboard and responsive review.
- **Evidence / confidentiality constraints:** Do not hide evidence gaps or promote section statuses to make the page appear complete. Do not invent content for missing sections. Preserve the [19-section template](CASE_STUDY_TEMPLATE.md).
- **Measurable acceptance criteria:**
  1. All 19 canonical sections and five nested sections remain reachable with their original IDs, titles and order; all missing/inapplicable explanations remain available.
  2. Every existing overview/checklist fragment resolves to the intended readable section, including targets inside disclosures.
  3. Keyboard-only checks reach and operate every changed disclosure with visible focus and no focus trap.
  4. At 320, 390, 768 and 1440px and actual 200% browser zoom, the affected content has no horizontal page overflow or clipped headings in open and closed states; results are recorded when tested.
- **Future implementation scope, if later authorized:** Checklist summaries/navigation and necessary scoped presentation changes on existing detail pages. No template deletion, route migration or status change.

## UX-05 — Reduce duplicated verification explanations

- **Priority:** P2
- **Status:** NOT STARTED
- **Problem / observation:** Verification limitations appear at several reading levels. Repetition may interrupt the story; removing it indiscriminately could conceal essential qualifications.
- **User value:** Readers can follow the contribution narrative and still understand provenance, evidence gaps and publication limits at relevant decision points.
- **Proposed improvement:** Inventory repeated explanations and consolidate redundant wording while preserving concise context at standalone entry points.
- **Dependencies:** Copy inventory across homepage, library, detail, related cards and metadata; status-meaning review; owner editorial approval and later implementation scope.
- **Evidence / confidentiality constraints:** Owner attestation must never become independent verification through shortened wording. Preserve the distinctions between reported artifacts, inspected evidence, acceptance, deployment, outcomes and permission. No status upgrade is authorized.
- **Measurable acceptance criteria:**
  1. A before/after inventory lists every proposed removal or rewrite and the retained location conveying the same required meaning.
  2. At least one redundant passage is consolidated, with exact duplicate-passage counts recorded before and after; no achieved reduction is claimed before review.
  3. Each affected standalone surface retains appropriate draft/source qualification; the detail page explicitly states unverified contributions, uninspected artifacts, unconfirmed outcomes and unknown publication permission.
  4. A factual comparison finds zero changed evidence/permission meanings or status values, and all newly used status links resolve.
- **Future implementation scope, if later authorized:** Shared and case-specific verification copy on existing surfaces, preserving required transparency and accessible naming.

## UX-07 — English/Vietnamese factual and terminology consistency

- **Priority:** P3
- **Status:** NOT STARTED
- **Problem / observation:** English is the only active runtime locale. Vietnamese drafts need revision-specific review before activation; translation does not itself establish factual parity or permission.
- **User value:** Readers in either language receive the same scope, contribution, evidence limitations and professional meaning.
- **Proposed improvement:** Compare paired drafts and resolve terminology, tense and factual differences before considering Vietnamese runtime activation.
- **Dependencies:** Stable English source revisions, complete proposed Vietnamese scope, an identified language reviewer, glossary decisions and language-specific permission review.
- **Evidence / confidentiality constraints:** Do not strengthen claims through translation or infer language proficiency. Preserve anonymous naming and the same evidence limits; permission for one text or language does not automatically cover another.
- **Measurable acceptance criteria:**
  1. A paired review covers every core page and selected featured narrative proposed for activation, with exact source and translation revisions recorded.
  2. Every factual claim, date, number, project identity, contribution boundary and status has an equivalent meaning or an explicitly resolved exception.
  3. All identified terminology discrepancies are resolved in a recorded glossary decision; zero unresolved factual mismatches remain in the proposed activation package.
  4. Reviewer role and review type are recorded honestly, including self-review where applicable; Vietnamese stays inactive until separate runtime authorization and readiness review.
- **Future implementation scope, if later authorized:** Reviewed translation content first. Locale routing, switching, metadata and activation require a separately approved technical scope.

## UX-08 — Permission-cleared BA artifact showcases

- **Priority:** P3
- **Status:** NOT STARTED
- **Problem / observation:** Artifact creation is reported for C2, but no real supporting artifact has been inspected for this portfolio. A showcase cannot yet substantiate a specific professional deliverable.
- **User value:** Hiring managers can inspect concrete analytical reasoning with transparent provenance and contribution boundaries.
- **Proposed improvement:** When relevant evidence becomes available, select a minimal permission-cleared excerpt with a readable explanation and traceability to the claim it supports.
- **Dependencies:** Available relevant artifact, exact revision/source, contribution review, disclosure authority, privacy clearance and separate showcase implementation authorization. Interactive artifacts additionally require their own authorization.
- **Evidence / confidentiality constraints:** Keep collection slots separate from actual artifact records. Review text, diagrams, screenshots, filenames and export metadata for direct/indirect identifiers and proprietary detail. Do not upload restricted originals. A new synthetic illustration must be labeled as such and cannot corroborate historical delivery.
- **Measurable acceptance criteria:**
  1. At least one proposed excerpt has an exact revision, source, personal/team contribution boundary, claim link and recorded review limitations.
  2. Permission covers the exact excerpt, caption, audience, channel and language; a recorded privacy review leaves zero unresolved disclosure findings before inclusion.
  3. The caption distinguishes authorship, inspection, acceptance, implementation and outcomes; only supported states are claimed.
  4. Each visual has a readable text explanation and, if later implemented, passes keyboard access, narrow-screen and 200% zoom checks without requiring access to a confidential original.
- **Future implementation scope, if later authorized:** A limited artifact presentation within an existing case and permitted assets. Downloads and interactivity require explicit scope; no automatic route creation or release.
