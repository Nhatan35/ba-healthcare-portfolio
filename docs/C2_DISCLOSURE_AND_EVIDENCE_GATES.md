## 6. Collection, missing evidence, and changes

Start with neutral metadata: which artifact types/revisions exist, who contributed, what they might demonstrate, and whether they can be reviewed. Prioritize one permitted flowchart plus one API-information clarification; request a timeline/report sample only to support the corresponding communication claim. No repository review, employer outreach, vendor contact, or evidence upload is performed in this phase.

Do not copy confidential meeting notes, real customer/patient information, call recordings, personal contact details, account identifiers, credentials, private endpoint URLs, API payloads or client workflow particulars into these documents or external tools. Record permitted summaries and references only. HRM prototypes, HRM module work and the credit-limit feature are outside C2 evidence scope; no claim is transferred between projects because a system label overlaps.

If an artifact cannot be reviewed, record that limitation and keep the statement owner-attested. Do not present unavailable evidence as inspected. A later authorized synthetic reconstruction would be labeled newly created and illustrative; it would not prove original delivery. No synthetic rule, requirement, payload or metric is introduced here.

For changed facts, artifact revisions, language revisions, permission restrictions or revocation, reopen affected claims and downstream card/detail/metadata/related-copy decisions. Track exact affected references; do not overwrite prior decisions. Withhold or remove affected published material only within an authorized later maintenance action. Planning rollback and application rollback do not erase the owner's identity confirmation.
