## 5. Focused collection questions

These questions guide future evidence collection; they do not specify system behavior or assume the answers:

1. When did the owner join, what had already been defined, and what was personally added or revised? What organization/context description can be shared?
2. Which specific MiniCRM-related workflows in HIS 315 fall within the owner's analysis? What are the boundaries of Inbound and Outbound? Which flowchart revisions correspond to each, and are they current-state, proposed-state, or another model?
3. Which API question did the owner clarify with Omicall, what information was requested from HIS, and who could confirm the requirement? Which system owns each relevant interaction, without assuming all endpoints belong to HIS 315? Can a non-confidential summary preserve the reasoning without exposing payloads or endpoints?
4. Which information ownership, access, exchange-failure, or exception questions actually arose? What remained unresolved? Do not introduce generic integration patterns as performed work.
5. Who used the timelines and weekly reports? What did the owner author versus compile from team updates? Does a progress status refer to analysis, development, testing, approval, or release?
6. What review, acceptance, implementation, or outcome evidence exists, and what is the owner permitted to disclose?

Start with E-OMI-01 and one E-OMI-02 flowchart, then link an actual E-OMI-03 clarification if permitted. E-OMI-04 supports communication/planning claims only to the extent its content and authorship support them. E-OMI-05 may clarify decision context without copying confidential meeting notes. E-OMI-06 is needed for any later delivery or results claim.
