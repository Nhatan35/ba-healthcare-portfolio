## Five verification statuses

Apply these to a claim or scoped content package at an exact revision, not to a person or their overall professional competence. The framework's definition may be complete while evidence for the owner's competency remains Missing.

| Status | Definition / entry condition | Exit criterion and responsible role | Public eligibility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Missing | Required facts, content or supporting evidence are absent. | Owner/author supplies a public-safe draft with known gaps recorded; move to Draft. | No. |
| Draft | A claim/content record exists with scope, attribution and sources or explicit gaps; not submitted as verified. | Author identifies exact revision and support, requests review; move to Needs Verification. | No. |
| Needs Verification | The specified revision awaits checking, has unresolved review issues, or was invalidated by a source change. This is not a claim that a reviewer is actively working. | BA reviewer and owner verify applicable claims and limits; relevant factual/privacy questions resolved. Reviewer identity and decision recorded; move to Verified or return for corrections. | No. |
| Verified | The stated claim's evidence, provenance, personal contribution and accuracy have passed scoped review. Does not establish disclosure permission, translation fluency, employment beyond the claim, or achieved operational benefits from a simulation. | Owner gathers applicable permission, privacy, language, completeness and readiness approvals at the same revisions; move to Approved for Publication. | Not sufficient alone. |
| Approved for Publication | A specified locale/package has verified claims, permitted disclosure, privacy review, language approval and complete content-readiness checks with recorded decision authority. | A later separately authorized release may publish that exact package after implementation checks. Changed/revoked evidence or approval reopens affected gates. | Content-eligible only; not automatically published or authorized to publish now. |

Assumptions, proposals and targets can be reviewed for correct labeling and reasoning; they cannot become verified real-world facts through workflow progression. A reviewer can confirm the statement “this is a proposed rule” without confirming the rule applies at a real organization. A simulation's verification covers origin, authored analysis and checks actually performed, never production outcomes.

Normal progression: Missing → Draft → Needs Verification → Verified → Approved for Publication. Return to Draft for rewriting, Missing where essential support is absent, or Needs Verification after a material change. Record the prior decision and reason; do not erase history. An overall package is held if any required claim/gate is unresolved; a verified optional claim cannot compensate for missing provenance or permission.

## Keep state dimensions separate

| Dimension | Vocabulary / meaning | Relationship to verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Provenance | Real / Simulated / Unverified | Unverified is unknown origin, not a sixth verification workflow state. Resolve it before presenting a released case. |
| Claim/content verification | Missing / Draft / Needs Verification / Verified / Approved for Publication | The five required states above. Approval scope must specify claim, locale and package revisions. |
| Locale editorial/translation | Missing / Draft / In review / Approved / Published / Needs review | Retains Phase 2.1 vocabulary. In review requires an assigned active reviewer; Approved requires exact source/fact revisions and language/privacy/fact checks. Published is recorded only after an actual authorized release. |
| Confidentiality | Unclassified / Public-safe after review / Restricted / Confidential | Portfolio handling categories, not legal classifications. Unknown source classification means treat as restricted until assessed. Keep restricted/confidential material outside the public repository and external tools. |
| Permission | Unknown / Pending / Granted / Denied / Revoked | Permission is scoped to specific claims, assets, locales and uses; expiry, if present, invalidates the grant. A factual claim may be Verified but not permitted for publication. |
| Release | Not released / Published / Withdrawn | Actual release history is separate from readiness. All current candidates are Not released. |

Crosswalk examples:

- A candidate planning outline exists, but case content/evidence is **Missing**, provenance **Unverified**, and permission **Unknown**. The outline's existence does not make the case Draft or Verified.
- A supported English draft can reach **Verified** claim status while English editorial state remains **In review** and permission **Pending**. It is not Approved for Publication.
- English editorial **Approved** and **Granted** permissions can support **Approved for Publication** only after all package gates pass. An untranslated Vietnamese variant remains **Missing**; English approval is not Vietnamese approval.
- A shared factual correction sends affected claims to **Needs Verification**, language variants to **Needs review**, and invalidates affected publication-readiness approval. A still-valid grant of permission does not verify the corrected claim.

No case or locale currently satisfies the approval examples; they define future behavior.

## Review workflow

1. **Intake:** owner confirms candidate provenance, industry/context, actual contribution and available evidence using public-safe descriptions. Assign owner and reviewer roles without implying appointments. Keep unknowns `[USER INPUT REQUIRED]`; do not request restricted originals.
2. **Map:** author records claim kinds, source limitations, exact artifact revisions, competencies, and need/goal/requirement/rule/criterion/validation links. Separate original artifacts from synthetic reconstructions.
3. **Draft English:** use the 20-element model and canonical 19-section sequence. Include concise overview and contribution notes; record missing/inapplicable evidence with reasons.
4. **Verify business and personal claims:** review source support, scope, attribution, methods, decisions and outcomes. Record what was inspected and what was not; source availability alone is not proof of the owner's authorship.
5. **Review confidentiality and permission:** review each prose/asset/export variant, disclosure authority and scope. Exclude restricted evidence; lack of permission blocks publication even if facts are correct.
6. **Approve English editorial baseline:** review clarity and consistency across case, homepage summary, About, Resume and metadata. Establish exact English/shared-fact/artifact revisions for localization.
7. **Localize and compare:** review Vietnamese fluency independently and compare factual meaning with English; preserve identifiers, modality and limits. Record reviewer and source revisions. No independent review is claimed unless it occurred.
8. **Assess content readiness:** verify package completeness, permissions, evidence and locale availability; owner records approval for a specified package. Publishing and technical checks remain later authorized work.
9. **Maintain:** reassess all dependent claims, summaries, resume facts, downloads, metadata and translations after changes or permission revocation. Keep an impact log and reopen gates selectively with recorded reasoning.

## Review criteria and required evidence

Use Pass / Fail / Pending / Not applicable with reason for each criterion. Pending is not Pass. These checklist outcomes are distinct from the five verification statuses.

| Dimension | Checks required before approval | Evidence / responsible role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business accuracy | Confirm setting, problem boundary, actors/handoffs, goals, actual versus hypothetical discovery, sources for root-cause reasoning, requirements quality, rule authority, exceptions, options and risk/dependency rationale. Clinical/regulatory/standards applicability claims need current authoritative sources and context; omit unsupported claims. | Source-linked claims, context/decision records and review limits; owner + BA reviewer, relevant domain authority if a claim requires it. No such external claim is established in this phase. |
| Personal contribution | Confirm role/timeframe, specific authored work, actual techniques and decision authority; distinguish individual analysis from team delivery and organizational results. Check resume and summaries against the same facts. | Permitted authorship/contribution evidence and owner confirmation; reviewer records verification limits. No title/seniority/experience inferred. |
| Supporting evidence | Inspect specific artifact/revision or permitted excerpt; identify what it supports and what it cannot. Check competency links, need-to-validation traceability, proposed versus executed status, and source limitations. | Claim/evidence register and traceability rows; BA reviewer. A diagram title, unexecuted criterion or inaccessible source is insufficient to prove delivery/outcomes. |
| Outcome and metric integrity | Separate targets, expected benefits, simulated checks and measured outcomes. Every reported metric has definition/unit, source, measurement period, baseline/comparator where relevant, method/calculation, scope, attribution and limitations. | Measurement/validation record with permitted evidence; owner + BA reviewer. Unknown is not zero, and unknown measurement history is not “not measured.” Omit unsupported metrics. |
| Healthcare confidentiality | Inspect narrative, screenshots, diagrams, tables, filenames, comments, hidden layers, metadata and exports for patient/employee identifiers, indirect identifiers, secrets and proprietary detail. Synthetic examples must not reproduce identifiable cases. | Per-asset privacy decision and limitations; owner + authorized disclosure reviewer. Replacing names or applying blur alone does not clear an artifact. No restricted material sent to external translation/AI tools. |
| English writing | Use clear specific prose, accurate tense/modality, expanded acronyms, concise summaries and descriptive links. Keep provenance, actual contribution, proposal/result boundaries and limitations visible. | Exact English revision and editorial review; English reviewer + owner for facts. No generic template filler or unsupported first-person claims. |
| Vietnamese localization | Check natural professional Vietnamese, diacritics, contextual vocabulary and glossary consistency. Compare role/seniority, scope, assumptions, numbers, dates, units, rules/criteria, source references, outcomes and caveats. Include captions, metadata, navigation, artifact language labels and resume facts. | Locale revision tied to English/fact revisions; bilingual BA-capable reviewer. Translation is not proof of the owner's language proficiency. |
| Publication readiness | Required sections accounted for; essential evidence chain available; no unresolved public placeholders; correct Real/Simulated label; contact/CV facts approved; all included assets/claims permitted; intended locale set complete; approvals match revisions. | Owner's package decision, reviewer findings and permission records. Actual release additionally needs separate authorization and later implementation/technical checks. |

If a source is withheld, disclose the limit and reduce the public claim to what permitted evidence supports. Do not state “independently verified” when only owner confirmation or a secondary description was reviewed. Public-safe methodological descriptions can be useful without exposing restricted originals or implying nonexistent corroboration.
