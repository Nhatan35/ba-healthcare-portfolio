# BA Competency Framework — Phase 2.2

Prepared 13 October 2026. Status: proposed evidence taxonomy, not a verified skills profile. The current owner request authorizes planning and documentation only. No professional competence, project participation, qualification, or result is established by this framework.

Read with the [evidence matrix](CASE_STUDY_EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md), [content architecture](CONTENT_ARCHITECTURE.md), and [verification checklist](CONTENT_VERIFICATION_CHECKLIST.md). The seven presentation groups from [Portfolio strategy](PORTFOLIO_STRATEGY.md) remain intact; the 14 competencies below provide finer evidence classification. This is a portfolio editorial taxonomy, not a certification assessment or external proficiency standard.

## Evidence conventions

- Candidate references: **C1** Healthcare Appointment Management; **C2** Healthcare Omnichannel Integration; **C3** Employee Shift Scheduling. All three have unverified provenance. C3's industry is also unverified.
- Candidate suitability below is a planning hypothesis. It does not mean an artifact exists, a technique was used, or the owner performed the work.
- All 14 competencies currently have evidence verification status **Missing**: `[USER INPUT REQUIRED]` for permitted authored evidence and contribution confirmation. An existing taxonomy is not evidence of personal competence.
- A reviewed original simulation may demonstrate a particular analytical technique. It cannot establish real stakeholder facilitation, employment, production integration, clinical expertise, or operational impact.
- Verify individual claims and artifact revisions rather than awarding a blanket “Verified” competency badge. Show a specific demonstrated example and its limitations; do not assign percentages, seniority, or skill scores.

## Competency-to-evidence contract

Each future evidence link records: competency ID; project ID; claim ID; artifact ID and revision; precise section/excerpt; evidence reference; actual personal contribution; real/simulated provenance; verification status; permission status; locale availability; and limitations. An artifact title without an inspectable explanation is insufficient.

Use this relationship, not an invented completed example:

`COMP → specific claim → authored artifact/revision → evidence reference → review decision`

Within the case, connect `need/problem → GOAL → REQ → BR (where applicable) → AC → validation record/status`. The [content architecture](CONTENT_ARCHITECTURE.md#traceability-and-evidence-records) defines identifiers and missing-link handling. Branches may share requirements or criteria; unrelated rules should not be inserted just to fill the chain.

Current coverage: 14 competencies defined, zero verified personal competency claims, three candidate case mappings, zero supplied artifacts or completed evidence chains. A mapping is a collection plan until supporting evidence arrives.
