## 5. Credit-limit feature: dedicated evidence workstream

H-05 records owner-attested analysis of a newly introduced feature, distinct from enhancement of existing HRM modules. It does not establish exact deliverables, business rules, approval authority, or outcomes. Do not reinterpret this as payroll deduction, employee lending, patient credit, insurance credit, a specific payer model, or a deployed financial integration. None of those details was supplied. Keep this workstream within HRM unless later evidence and the owner justify a different case boundary.

These are **proposed clarification questions**, not requirements, policies, or assertions about the system:

1. What does “postpaid credit limit” mean in this project, who is affected, and what business need prompted it? Which terms and names may be disclosed?
2. What did the owner personally analyze, write, clarify, review, or coordinate? Which work was inherited or performed by others?
3. What roles do HIS 315 and HRM play? Which system holds authoritative debt, limit, and payment information, if applicable? What directions of interaction were agreed?
4. Who defines and approves eligibility, limit changes, payment application, and exceptions? Are these rules documented, or still being discussed?
5. If payment and debt states differ between systems, what behavior has actually been agreed? Are rejection, reversal, delayed exchange, or duplicate events relevant? Do not assume they are implemented.
6. Which requirements and acceptance criteria exist, who reviewed them, and what validation or release evidence can be disclosed?

Collect E-CR-01 first, then a permitted, bounded analysis sample under E-CR-02. Request E-CR-03 only to establish what review/testing/release evidence actually exists. Use no real balances, employee/patient identifiers, account details, credentials, or private API payloads. A later authorized synthetic illustration must be labeled as newly created and cannot prove original delivery.
